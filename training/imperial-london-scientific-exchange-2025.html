Scientific exchange at Imperial College London

By Syed Adnan Haider

When
18–21 November 2025
Where
Imperial College London · United Kingdom
My role
International meeting

Polio Sequencing Consortium meeting

I took part in the Polio Sequencing Consortium meeting at Imperial College London, contributing to technical exchange with the international surveillance community.

The discussions covered laboratory implementation, quality assurance, data reporting and bioinformatics. This was a scientific meeting and exchange connected to my public-health work at NIH Islamabad.

International exchange provides an opportunity to discuss shared implementation challenges and learn from colleagues working in different settings. The photographs record the London visit and meeting.
