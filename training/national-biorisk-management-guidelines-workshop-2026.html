Professional contribution · National consultation

Contributing to national bio-risk management guidelines.

Technical Expert

By Syed Adnan Haider · 16–18 September 2026 · Islamabad, Pakistan

My contribution

Technical review with laboratory practice in mind.

I contributed as a Technical Expert at the National Consultative Workshop to finalize the National Bio-Risk Management Guidelines for Laboratories, held on 16–18 September 2026.

My contribution involved actively participating in technical deliberations, critically reviewing the proposed guidance, and sharing practical insights and recommendations to strengthen its relevance and applicability in laboratory settings.

A key focus was on translating biosafety, biosecurity, and risk-based management principles into practical guidance that can support safer and more consistent laboratory practices across Pakistan.
