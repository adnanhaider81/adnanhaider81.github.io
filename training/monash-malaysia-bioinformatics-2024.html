Training received

Next Generation Sequencing Bioinformatics Training

I participated in an intensive NGS bioinformatics workshop at Monash University Malaysia, organized with Wellcome Connecting Science and the Genome Institute of Singapore.

The workshop combined practical genomic data analysis, collaborative problem-solving and international peer learning. I attended as a participant, contributing to discussions and exercises with the workshop cohort.

This professional development sits alongside my teaching and mentoring work in Pakistan. The photographs show both a workshop discussion and the wider group, with the completion certificate linked below.
