About

Syed Adnan Haider

Clinical and pathogen genomics, connected by sequencing and interpretation.

I am Syed Adnan Haider, a Research Scientist in the Department of Virology, National Institute of Health, Islamabad, Pakistan. I have more than ten years of experience across clinical sequencing, pathogen genomics and bioinformatics.

My work connects laboratory methods with computational analysis and genomic interpretation, spanning inherited disorders and cancer through to infectious-disease surveillance and outbreak investigations.

Experience across human and pathogen genomics

Clinical genomics · Rehman Medical Institute · October 2015–January 2021

Whole-exome sequencing, targeted panels and variant interpretation

As a Scientific Officer at the Center for Genomic Sciences, I worked on clinical sequencing for inherited disorders and cancer. My responsibilities combined laboratory work, bioinformatic analysis, germline and somatic variant review, and clinician-facing reporting supported by reference databases and published literature.

I contributed to research on thalassemia, familial deafness and oral cancer, as well as microbiome research comparing 16S amplicon and shallow shotgun sequencing. I also maintained SOPs and quality records and trained colleagues in sequencing and data analysis.

Pathogen genomics · NIH Islamabad · April 2021–present

Genomic surveillance and outbreak investigations

I joined NIH during the COVID-19 response and helped establish and operate sequencing workflows for viral genomic surveillance. My work now spans molecular biology, sequencing, reproducible bioinformatics and interpretation for emerging, re-emerging and other priority pathogens, using clinical and environmental samples.

My current and published contributions include poliovirus, influenza, RSV, SARS-CoV-2, dengue and other viruses. I develop documented analysis workflows, contribute to sequencing collaborations and maintain Pakistan's public SARS-CoV-2 Nextstrain Community build.

Education and scientific development

I hold an M.Phil. and a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Biotechnology and Genetic Engineering from the University of Agriculture, Peshawar. My professional development has included training in genomic epidemiology, sequencing, reproducible analysis and the design of scientific teaching.

I also teach and mentor colleagues in sequencing-data analysis and public-health bioinformatics. My approach is to make the reasoning behind an analysis as clear as the output itself. Explore my teaching and training record.

What I want to understand next

I am interested in how genomic evidence can better explain human disease and the spread of infectious pathogens. In clinical genomics, that means careful interpretation of sequence variation in its biological and clinical context. In pathogen genomics, it means understanding the roles of evolution, population immunity, exposure and sampling in the patterns we observe.

I welcome conversations about doctoral research, scientific roles, training and collaborations across clinical genomics, pathogen genomics, molecular diagnostics and bioinformatics.
