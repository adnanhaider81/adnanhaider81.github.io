Teaching Approach

Designed for practical independence.

Training is most useful when participants can connect commands, quality controls, outputs, and reporting decisions into one understandable workflow.

Start with the command line

Make file structure, environments, inputs, outputs, and reproducible commands understandable.

Connect QC to interpretation

Show how read quality, depth, controls, mapping, and masking shape a defensible result.

Teach the complete workflow

Move from raw data to a traceable report rather than teaching disconnected software commands.

Collaborate

Need practical genomics or bioinformatics training?

Available for workshops, curriculum development, workflow demonstrations, and collaborative capacity building in pathogen genomics and public-health bioinformatics.
