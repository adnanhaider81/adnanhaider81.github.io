Why this series

I started at the bench, too.

I came into bioinformatics from a wet-lab background. Learning to connect the data I generated with the work needed to understand it shaped how I now teach.

In this series, I explain what we are doing, why it matters, and how each step connects to the next. The exercises begin with a working environment and basic files before moving into analysis.
