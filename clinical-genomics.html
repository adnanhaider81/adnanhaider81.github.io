01 / Laboratory & analysis

Reading beyond the variant list.

At RMI, my responsibilities included laboratory workflows, sequencing-data analysis, germline and somatic variant review, and clinician-facing reporting. I used reference databases and published literature to place sequence findings in their biological and clinical context.

The work also involved maintaining SOPs and quality records, and training colleagues in sequencing and analysis. This experience informs how I approach genomic evidence today: sequence quality, the question being asked and the limits of interpretation need to remain visible throughout an analysis.

A closer look · Familial deafness

From a gene panel to candidate variants.

In a study of two Pakistani brothers with familial deafness, an inherited-disease panel and Illumina MiSeq sequencing were used to investigate candidate variants. My contribution was data analysis. The case study explains the published workflow and the distinction between identifying candidates and establishing a diagnosis.
